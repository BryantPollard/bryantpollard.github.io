Climate readingc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://bryantpollard.github.io/neon-epn/ontology#ClimateReading

One measurement from the climate telemetry. Native-resolution readings carry sosa:resultTime (an instant). Daily aggregates additionally carry sosa:phenomenonTime pointing at a time:Interval spanning the day, because a daily mean is not observed at an instant. Results use QUDT QuantityValue (value + unit), or sosa:hasSimpleResult in lean mode.
has super-classes
Observation c
is disjoint with
Capture event c, Drag sample c, Pathogen test c, Phenophase observation c, Vector occurrence c

IRI: https://bryantpollard.github.io/neon-epn/ontology#PhenophaseObservation

Plant individualc back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://bryantpollard.github.io/neon-epn/ontology#PlantIndividual

A marked plant observed repeatedly through the season, identified by the contributing network's individual identifier. Unlike a host animal it is sessile, so its location is fixed and it is revisited on a schedule rather than recaptured by chance. Identifiers are network-scoped: a NEON individualID and a USA-NPN individual_id may collide numerically and are kept in separate IRI spaces.
has super-classes
Feature Of Interest c
is in domain of
of plant taxon op

Tick samplec back to ToC or Class ToC

IRI: https://bryantpollard.github.io/neon-epn/ontology#TickSample

A tick specimen collected by drag-cloth sampling at a plot and submitted for pathogen testing, identified by NEON subsampleID. In every NEON record examined (231,892 across 39 sites) individualCount is 1, so a sample is a single tick; the count is retained because the field permits pooling in principle. Unlike a host individual, a tick sample is an environmental sample with no tagged organism, so vector-borne detections attach to place rather than to an animal.
has super-classes
Feature Of Interest c
is in domain of
collected at op, individual count dp
is disjoint with
Host individual c
