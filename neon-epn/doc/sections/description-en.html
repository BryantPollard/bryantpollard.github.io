NEON-EPN Ontology: National Ecological Observatory Network Eco-Pathogen Network: Description back to ToC

Semantic layer (TBox) for the NEON-EPN capstone project. Models NEON host-pathogen sampling events and continuous climate sensor telemetry over a dynamic spatial hierarchy, as specializations of the W3C SOSA observation pattern. Serves as the single schema that governs RDF ingestion (Stage 2), SHACL validation (Stage 3), SPARQL serving (Stage 4), and the flattened Temporal Graph Benchmark export (Stage 5). Reuse policy: SOSA is imported in full - Observation, Sampling, FeatureOfInterest, Sensor, resultTime and hasResult are all load-bearing here. Everything else is soft-referenced by IRI (MIREOT-style) rather than imported: GeoSPARQL, QUDT, NCBITaxon, ENVO, the Plant Phenology Ontology (PPO, CC BY 3.0), and the three OWL-Time terms below, and PROV-O for the contributing network. Phenophase individuals carry skos:closeMatch links to PPO, which was designed to accommodate both NEON and USA-NPN status-based monitoring; that shared vocabulary is what allows observations from the two networks to be queried together without a bespoke crosswalk. Importing OWL-Time in full pulled in Duration, GeneralDurationDescription, DateTimeDescription, TRS and a dozen other classes that no instance data ever used, tripling the apparent size of the model for no benefit.
